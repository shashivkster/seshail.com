FitDiary

Training logs a coach has actually verified.

Most fitness apps take the user's word for it. That is fine when the log is for your own motivation, and useless the moment it needs to persuade somebody else — a selector, an academy, a sponsor.

FitDiary separates the two. A player records their training as they would anywhere else, and a partner coach or academy validates the entries afterwards. A validated history is worth something a self-reported one is not. Partner gyms, fitness studios and sports nutrition stores attach discounts to targets, so hitting them pays off immediately as well as eventually.
